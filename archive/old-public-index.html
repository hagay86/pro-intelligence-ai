How it works

Your chat becomes the curriculum

You don't write a course. You teach the way you already teach — and Synapse turns it into a permanent, queryable mentor.

1

Create your room

Sign up as a professional. Name your cohort, get a 6-character invite code, share it with students. Takes 30 seconds.

2

Just teach

Drop knowledge in the group chat, answer student questions, share frameworks. The AI is quietly absorbing everything in real time.

3

Students ask the AI

When you're offline, students hit ✨ Ask AI and get answers in your voice — grounded in your actual messages, with attribution.

What it does

Built for cohort-based teaching

🧠

Grounded answers, not hallucinated ones

The AI is forbidden from making things up. If a topic isn't in your chat, it says so. If it answers, it cites who said what.

📚

Auto-organized knowledge

One click and the AI re-reads the entire chat to produce structured notes — topics, frameworks, open questions, gaps it noticed.

🎓

Real cohorts, real invites

Create rooms, share invite codes, see your roster. Students can only access rooms they've been invited to.

✨

AI in the group chat

Toggle Ask-AI mode and the response appears in the chat so the whole cohort learns together — not just the asker.

🔐

Your data, your machine

Synapse runs on your computer with SQLite. No third-party SaaS holding your students' conversations.

⚡

Public URL when you want one

One command (npm run tunnel) and your students can join from anywhere.
