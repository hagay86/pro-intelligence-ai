# Outreach playbook — getting to Anthropic

A practical playbook for actually getting your pitch in front of someone at Anthropic who can do something about it. Read top-to-bottom, work through it in order.

## Channel priority

Try these in this order:

| Priority | Channel | Effort | Likelihood of response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Anthropic for Startups application** | 30 min | High (within 4 weeks) |
| 2 | **Warm intro via an Israeli VC** | 2-3 days of asking | Very high if you find the right intro |
| 3 | **LinkedIn DM to a real human** | 15 min each | Medium |
| 4 | **Cold email to startups@ / business@** | 10 min | Low–medium |
| 5 | **Twitter DM to Anthropic team members** | 5 min | Low, risky if done badly |

Do channel 1 immediately. Start working on channel 2 in parallel.

---

## Channel 1 — Anthropic for Startups (do this today)

**Where**: https://www.anthropic.com/startups

**What it gives you**: API credits, sometimes intros, listing as a startup using Claude.

**What they're looking for**:
- Real product, not just an idea
- Clear use case that demonstrates Claude's strengths
- Sustainable usage growth (not a one-time experiment)

**Application notes**:
- Use the language from `PITCH.md` as your starting answers
- Lead with the **insight** (AI-relationship-as-product), not the product features
- Be specific about credits needed — "$25,000 to support 90-day Israel launch" beats "as much as possible"
- Mention you already use Claude Code subscription auth — most applicants don't, this stands out

**Expected timeline**: 2–4 weeks for response. Yes/no — and if yes, credits typically come within a week of approval.

## Channel 4 — Cold email

**Addresses to try** (in order):

1. `startups@anthropic.com` — startups program (use this even if you applied via form)
2. `business@anthropic.com` — general business inquiries
3. `support@anthropic.com` — last resort, often forwards

**Subject lines that work** (test one):
- "Hebrew-native creator-AI platform built on Claude Code subscription auth"
- "Synapse — turning Claude relationships into creator products (Israel launch)"
- "Pre-launch feedback: novel Claude use case, would love your eye"

**Email body** (short — 3 paragraphs max):

> Hi Anthropic team,
>
> Building Synapse: the first product that turns a creator's relationship with Claude into a sellable asset for their community. Israel-first, Hebrew-native, built on Claude Code subscription auth (not raw API keys — sophisticated integration of your stack).

> hagaydardik@gmail.com · +972-[number]

Anthropic team members on X who sometimes engage with thoughtful DMs:

- **@DarioAmodei** — CEO. Don't DM unless you've actually done something impressive. Reads but rarely replies to cold messages.
- **@AnthropicAI** — official account, monitored by team. DMs get reviewed.
- **@DevRel folks** — Devrel team members at Anthropic often respond on Twitter
- **Anthropic engineers** — many are active, but DM only if you have a specific technical question

**Best Twitter strategy isn't DMs — it's tweeting about your product** in a way Anthropic team is likely to see:
- Tweet your demo with `@AnthropicAI` tagged
- Show a video of the Hebrew product working
- Quote-tweet Anthropic announcements with relevant additions
- Build presence so when you DM, you're not a stranger

**Don't**: spam @DarioAmodei with cold pitches. Founders do this and it backfires.

## Follow-up rhythm

After initial outreach, the rhythm should be:

- **Day 0**: send initial outreach
- **Day 7**: if no reply, one short bump ("hey, just floating this up — happy to wait, just wanted to make sure it didn't get lost")
- **Day 21**: if still no reply, one final "Closing the loop — last try, then I'll stop bothering you. Here's our progress update: [1 sentence on traction]. Open if there's interest."

Then stop. Don't burn bridges by becoming the founder who emails forever.

## What needs to be true before you start

Before you send a single email, have these ready:

1. ✅ **`PITCH.md` finalized** — done, in this repo
2. ⚠ **Working demo at a public URL** — your index.html runs locally; deploy it to Vercel/Netlify so anyone can click and see it work. **Do this first**, before any outreach. A demo behind a localhost URL kills momentum.
3. ⚠ **Founder LinkedIn polished** — make sure it positions you as an AI builder, not generic
4. ⚠ **3–5 pilot creators identified by name** — even if you haven't signed them yet, having "I'm in conversations with X, Y, Z" makes the pitch real
5. ⚠ **Founder email signature** — looks professional. Include the demo URL.

Items 2-5 are blockers. Don't skip them to feel productive — they'll kill your conversion rate by 5x.

## Realistic expectations

| Outcome | Probability | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic for Startups acceptance + credits | 40-60% | 2-4 weeks |
| One real warm intro from an Israeli VC | 30-50% if you ask 5+ VCs | 2-6 weeks |
| Conversation with a Solutions or BD person | 10-25% | 4-8 weeks |
| Investment from Anthology Fund | <5% at this stage | 3-6 months if pursued |
| Formal "partnership" | <2% at this stage | Not realistic |
| "Powered by Claude" branding approval | High if you ask politely | 1-2 weeks |

If you get the first two within 8 weeks, that's a successful outreach effort. Use them to build into the next round.

---

## After traction

Once you have **10 real creators each running a real community on Synapse**, everything changes. You re-pitch the same people with metrics, and the responses are different. The goal of this first round of outreach is *not* the partnership — it's to plant the seed and get the credits so you can build to traction faster.

Treat round one as a relationship-opener. The real pitch comes in round two, with numbers.
