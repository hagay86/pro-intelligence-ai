# Synapse — One-Pager for Anthropic

> The first product that turns a creator's relationship with Claude into a sellable asset.

## The insight

Claude is now a commodity — anyone can subscribe for $20/month. The scarce resource has shifted: it's the **human who knows how to instruct Claude well**. A creator who has spent hundreds of hours teaching Claude their frameworks, voice, decision patterns, and edge cases is dramatically more valuable than a generic Claude user — yet today there's no way to package and sell that relationship.

Synapse is the first product designed around this gap.

## The product

Synapse is a group-chat platform where one creator/expert leads a community and Claude serves as the community's resident AI mentor — instructed by the creator, grounded in the creator's actual conversations, and capable of navigating the entire community's chat history as a queryable knowledge base.

The flow:
1. A creator (digital advertising expert, fitness coach, software architect, whatever) connects their personal Claude account via Claude Code subscription auth.
2. Synapse imports relevant conversation history as system context.
3. Their community joins via a 6-character invite code and chats — with the creator, with each other, and with the Claude instance now grounded in the creator's style and the community's history.
4. Members can ask the AI anything ("where did people discuss retargeting last month?") and get specific messages back with attribution.

The community chat **compounds in value** over time — every message makes the AI smarter for the next question.

## Why now

- **Creator economy bottleneck**: creators max out on time. Every solo expert with 1,000+ followers wants to scale themselves without writing a course or hiring a team.
- **Community pain point**: Telegram and Discord communities have hit a scale wall — too many messages, no findability, the host has zero leverage. Channels are noise, not assets.
- **Claude's specific strengths make this possible now**: long context (whole community history fits), instruction following (creator's voice transfers cleanly), and accuracy (grounded answers cite sources, no hallucination).
